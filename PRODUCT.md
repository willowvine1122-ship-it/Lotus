_Written from the founder's brief and the research doc (/mnt/project-files/research/ghl-competitor-findings.md). Facts marked (inferred) were not confirmed directly._

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: the Lotus agency team (founders, CMO, account managers, media buyers). Situation: start of the working day and the internal client meeting; they need to know, per client, what happened yesterday and what needs action, and to read each other's call notes. Secondary: client staff who log into their own sub-account to see their results (inferred: read-mostly).

## Product Purpose

Lotus is the agency's own operating system, replacing GoHighLevel plus Housecall Pro plus Fundraise Up. It runs every client in one place: CRM, inbox, funnels, automations, payments, and a Daily Diagnostic that flags which client needs attention. Success: the team opens one screen each morning and knows exactly where every client stands, and clients get a cleaner experience than any competitor.

## Positioning

One platform that runs both kinds of client the agency serves: digital marketing (ads, leads, booked appointments, CPA) and digital fundraising (donors, donations, recurring giving). Competitors do one or the other. The Daily Diagnostic with shared internal notes is unique to Lotus.

## Operating Context

Agency account on top, one sub-account per client, each sub-account in marketing or fundraising mode. Morning review, internal client meetings where the CMO's call notes are read aloud, daily ad buying, weekly client reports. Data feeds: Meta Ads, Google Ads, Stripe donations (planned).

## Capabilities and Constraints

Built: agency workspace with Daily Diagnostic board, client list, client detail with notes and tasks; Supabase schema with row-level security per sub-account. Planned: CRM, inbox, booking, funnels, automations, donation checkout, donor CRM. Terminology: "client" (sub-account), "Daily Diagnostic", "mode" (marketing / fundraising), health "green / amber / red". Undecided: client-facing white-label depth.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Lotus. The founder's standing requirement: the UI/UX must be far cleaner, more organized and more advanced than GoHighLevel.

Binding reference (2026-10-09): the founder shared screenshots of a tool they consider beautiful, a "Daily Diagnostic" from a product called workfuel (private; copies in the project files under lotus/reference/). Its craft level is the bar and its organisation is the standing preference for the Daily Diagnostic: a KPI strip across the top (active clients with spend, leads with avg cost, appointments with avg cost, avg revenue with ROI), a dense sortable client table (client with status dot and badges, lead cost with delta and count, appointment cost, book rate, budget pacing gauge with budget, spend, estimated ROI, season/trend bar), and a persistent right-hand client panel (header with status, tags, location, time, assignees; 2x2 metric tiles; spend, pacing and estimated revenue rows; rolling 7-day performance line chart; lead and appointment volume bars; year-over-year; 7D/30D trend tiles; month-by-month metrics table; a notes feed with avatars). The fundraising mode mirrors it with raised, donors, average gift, recurring, and raised by country.

## Evidence on Hand

No real client data yet. All client names, numbers and notes in the app are demo data and must stay labelled as such. No logo asset yet.

## Product Principles

- One glance tells you who needs attention; everything else is a click away.
- Fewer things, done precisely. GoHighLevel's failure is bloat and clutter.
- Internal and client views share one truth but not one audience.
- Speed and calm: the tool disappears into the task.
